## Overview

Sparse Projection Oblique Randomer Forests (SPORF) extends the random forest framework with oblique splits and sparse projections, achieving competitive performance with deep networks while maintaining interpretability and computational efficiency.

## Your Contribution

In this work, I played a key engineering role by:
- **Library Development & Maintenance**: Co-developed and maintained the R and Python implementations, published to CRAN and PyPI
- **Issue Resolution**: Actively managed and resolved user-reported issues across both language implementations
- **Experimental Validation**: Designed and executed experiments to validate algorithm performance
- **Figure Generation**: Produced key visualizations for manuscript publication

This work demonstrates the importance of accessible, well-maintained software in democratizing advanced ML methods.

## Key Findings

- Oblique splits in random forests improve generalization without substantially increasing complexity
- Sparse projections reduce dimensionality and computational cost
- Performance is competitive with deep networks on several benchmarks while maintaining interpretability

## Impact

SPORF provides practitioners with a practical, interpretable alternative to black-box deep learning for structured data problems.
